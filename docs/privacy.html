Papercrane Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

In short: Papercrane has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your scans, PDFs and recognized text are created and stored on your device. We do not run servers that receive your documents, and we never see them.

This policy explains what information the Papercrane mobile app (“Papercrane”, “the app”, “we”) handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app on iOS and Android.

1. Your documents stay on your device

  • Scanning: Pages are captured and cropped using the document scanner built into your phone’s operating system (Apple VisionKit on iOS, Google ML Kit Document Scanner on Android). Images are processed on the device.
  • Text recognition (OCR): Text is recognized on the device using Apple Vision (iOS) or Google ML Kit (Android). Your images and their text are not sent to us or used to train any model.
  • Storage: Page images, document names and recognized text are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. PDFs are created on the device when you export.
  • Sharing: When you share a PDF or text, it is sent only to the app or person you choose (for example Mail, WhatsApp or Files). That app’s own privacy policy then applies.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan documents when you tap “Scan”.
  • Photos: used only to import the images you pick when you tap “Import”. The app does not browse or upload your photo library.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. Purchases (Papercrane Pro)

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. To check whether you have an active Pro purchase and to let you restore it, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not your name or email);
  • your purchase and subscription history for Papercrane (product, price, dates, store transaction IDs);
  • basic technical data such as device type, operating-system version, app version, country/currency and IP address.

This data is used only to provide and verify purchases. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy, Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

4. Information sent by built-in Google components (Android)

On Android, Google ML Kit (used for scanning and text recognition) may contact Google servers to download models and updates, and may send Google anonymous metrics about the performance and usage of these features (for example device model, OS version and whether a feature succeeded). According to Google, this does not include your images or recognized text. See Google ML Kit terms.

5. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

6. Backups

If you use iCloud Backup (iOS) or Google device backup (Android), your operating system may include Papercrane’s stored documents in those backups. Backups are controlled by Apple or Google under your account settings, not by us.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete any document inside the app; its pages and text are removed from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.
  • To ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records linked to your anonymous app user ID, contact us (see below). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.

8. Children

Papercrane is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
